# EpiFive automation

Scheduled jobs for [EpiFive](https://www.epifive.com): the daily article and news brief, social video shares, the email digest, syndication, community drafts and IndexNow pings.

The jobs are started by a Cloudflare Worker scheduler. Each run checks out the site repository, does its work and pushes the result there; Cloudflare Pages then deploys the site.

## Launch radar (`radar/`, `radar.yml`)

Watches official product feeds (`radar/sources.json`: OpenAI, Microsoft 365, Tech Community, Windows, Google Workspace, Gemini, Android, Apple, WhatsApp, Meta, Anthropic, GitHub, RBI) and publishes a same-day step-by-step how-to when a big launch clears the score threshold. Security incidents: sources with `"category": "incident"` (The Hacker News, BleepingComputer, GitHub Blog security, Microsoft Security Blog, ThePrint Tech, MediaNama) are scored by `scoreIncident` (boosts major platforms such as GitHub, Microsoft 365, Google and npm/PyPI, and Indian banks, UPI and Aadhaar; requires a concrete user action) and become a same-day "what happened / am I affected / what to do now / how to prevent it" guide, within the same daily cap. It has no cron: it starts when the scheduler requests the Daily article or Social media workflow (the scheduler's existing 5 daily slots). At most 2 fast-track articles and 2 updates a day; official sources only; a draft that fails the quality gate gets one fix attempt, then goes to the site backlog as "(fast-track, failed gate)". Logs: `radar/log/<YYYY-MM>.md` (every scan: items, scores, decisions) and `radar/results.jsonl` (every write). Writer instructions live in the site repo at `automation/RADAR.md`. Tests: `node --test radar/radar.test.mjs radar/alert.test.mjs`.

Manual runs: Actions > Launch radar > Run workflow (`dry_run` drafts without publishing; `force_url` fast-tracks one official URL now; `force_category: incident` writes it as an incident guide).

Trending videos: when a new outage or incident guide, or a fast-track article scoring 12+, is published, the write job starts `social.yml` (slug, force, a first-second-hook brief) and `longvideo.yml` (slug, force, `publish_now`, upload) with GITHUB_TOKEN: the Short and Reels go out at once as always, and the long video is public on YouTube and the Facebook Page at once instead of 14:00 UTC. At most 1 pair a UTC day (`autoVideo` in `radar/results.jsonl`); consumer topics (online-safety, mobiles, laptops-hardware, how-to-tips) get the Short only (`autoVideoPlan` in `radar/lib.mjs`). A trending long video made before the 05:13 UTC daily run replaces that day's daily video. `dry_run` prints the commands without starting anything.

Email alerts: readers sign up in the "Outage alerts" box near the top of outage guides (or "Security alerts" after incident guides) on www.epifive.com, which puts them in the Resend segment `Alerts: outage` / `Alerts: security` (and the weekly newsletter). When the write job publishes a NEW outage or incident guide and the page is live, `radar.mjs alert` sends one short Resend broadcast to that segment (subject, what is down, the "Status as of" sentence, the first thing to do, the link, unsubscribe, postal footer). 1 alert per incident (radar key, slug, or outage platform family within 3 days), at most 2 in 24 hours of which at most 1 security alert, never for an update or a resolved outage (`alertGate` in `radar/alert.mjs`). Every decision is logged in `radar/results.jsonl` with `"type":"alert"`. Secrets: `RESEND_API_KEY`; optional `ALERT_POSTAL_ADDRESS` (full postal address for the footer). Preview without sending: `node radar/radar.mjs alert --dry-run --site <site checkout> --task task.json --status published --file content/posts/<slug>.md`.
