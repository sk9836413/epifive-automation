# EpiFive automation

Scheduled jobs for [EpiFive](https://www.epifive.com): the daily article and news brief, social video shares, the email digest, syndication, community drafts and IndexNow pings.

The jobs are started by a Cloudflare Worker scheduler. Each run checks out the site repository, does its work and pushes the result there; Cloudflare Pages then deploys the site.

## Daily article, refreshes and internal links (`article.yml`)

One run does one job and then starts the next run until the day's work is done (owner growth plan, 21 Nov 2026): new articles until `ARTICLES_PER_DAY` (repo variable, default 1) are published; then up to `REFRESH_PER_DAY` (repo variable, default 2) updates of existing posts from "## Refresh" in the site's `automation/backlog.md` (site `automation/WRITER.md` section 6; a failed refresh is moved to the end of the queue and still counts); then one internal-links pass (site `scripts/seo/interlink.mjs`: "See also" lists, orphans first, at most 40 posts a day). On Mondays (IST) the pass covers every post and `scripts/seo/refresh-queue.mjs gsc` rebuilds the Search Console "striking distance" block at the top of the refresh queue (`GSC_*` secrets). Every job logs a line in the site's `automation/log.md` (`published`, `refreshed`, `refresh-failed`, `interlink`); the daily report lists refreshes and the links pass separately from new articles. Manual runs: input `mode` (auto, article, refresh, interlink, interlink-full) and `dry_run`.

## Launch radar (`radar/`, `radar.yml`)

Watches official product feeds (`radar/sources.json`: OpenAI, Microsoft 365, Tech Community, Windows, Google Workspace, Gemini, Android, Apple, WhatsApp, Meta, Anthropic, GitHub, RBI) and publishes a same-day step-by-step how-to when a big launch clears the score threshold. Security incidents: sources with `"category": "incident"` (The Hacker News, BleepingComputer, GitHub Blog security, Microsoft Security Blog, ThePrint Tech, MediaNama) are scored by `scoreIncident` (boosts major platforms such as GitHub, Microsoft 365, Google and npm/PyPI, and Indian banks, UPI and Aadhaar; requires a concrete user action) and become a same-day "what happened / am I affected / what to do now / how to prevent it" guide, within the same daily cap. It has no cron: it starts when the scheduler requests the Daily article or Social media workflow (the scheduler's existing 5 daily slots). At most 2 fast-track articles and 2 updates a day; official sources only; a draft that fails the quality gate gets one fix attempt, then goes to the site backlog as "(fast-track, failed gate)". Logs: `radar/log/<YYYY-MM>.md` (every scan: items, scores, decisions) and `radar/results.jsonl` (every write). Writer instructions live in the site repo at `automation/RADAR.md`. Tests: `node --test radar/radar.test.mjs radar/alert.test.mjs`.

Tiers (`radar/brief.mjs`, since 14 Nov 2026; about 90 sources incl. enterprise software, cloud, AI labs and model changelogs, chips, device makers, and aggregators as signals only; fetch status per source in `sources.json`, blocked ones in `notUsed`):
- Tier 1: the fast-track article above, caps unchanged (2 a day + 1 major-outage extra). Score floors for a flagship device (iPhone, Galaxy S/Z, Pixel) from the maker's newsroom, a new flagship AI model from OpenAI/Anthropic/Google/Meta, a big AI price drop, and an AI model retirement due within 120 days. One fast-track per device or model across sources (story key).
- Tier 2: every relevant item on one page per IST day, `radar/live/<date>.json` (+ `index.json`), served by the site's Pages Functions at https://www.epifive.com/news/live/<date> (no rebuild) and `/sitemap/live.xml`. 2-4 lines per item from one batched `claude -p --model haiku --tools ""` call per scan, kept only if every number appears in the official text (`validateLines`), else template lines from the official text. Same story across sources and 3 days = one item (official replaces "reported"); per-source `briefMax`, 80 a day. IndexNow at most hourly when the page changed.
- Tier 3: an official device launch (maker's newsroom) starts `devices.yml` with `force` and focus `Launch mode: ...` (site `automation/DEVICES.md`), at most 2 runs a day, 3 devices a run (`state.devices`).
Tests: `node --test radar/brief.test.mjs`.

Trending articles (owner, 20 Nov 2026: "ye sab trending topics hote h, chhootne nahi chahiye"; `radar/trending.mjs` `trendingArticles`, `missedTrends`): a trending story (Techmeme top headline or 3+ reputable outlets) that is a product/AI launch, AI/cloud/security/major-platform news or a payments/banking/telecom outage, and that no post, backlog line or radar item covers, takes a fast-track slot while today's slots last, else it is parked at the top of the site's `automation/backlog.md` under "## Radar trending" (an HTML comment with the outlets and links, then `slug | title | topic | reader goal`). An official radar item about the same story that scored "below" is lifted instead. Business, finance, policy and legal stories (earnings, funding, stock moves, IPOs, leadership, lawsuits) stay news-page only. At most `settings.maxTrendingArticlesPerDay` (4) a day, trending and missed-check parks together. A fast-track the writer skipped with any reason but "covered by" / "news only" (the write job records the RADAR-SKIP line from `.radar-skip.txt`) is parked there too. Once a day (first scan from 18:30 IST, or `scan --missed-check`) the "Missed-trends check" in the radar log re-checks the last 48 hours (trending stories over the cap, vendor-feed items scoring >= threshold - 2, skipped or failed fast-tracks) against the posts and backlog and parks what is still uncovered; the daily report email lists it. Vertical AI launches ("Gemini for healthcare", "ChatGPT for Healthcare", "Claude for Financial Services") get +2 and, from an official feed, always clear the threshold. Tests: `node --test radar/trending-articles.test.mjs`.

Manual runs: Actions > Launch radar > Run workflow (`dry_run` drafts without publishing; `force_url` fast-tracks one official URL now; `force_category: incident` writes it as an incident guide).

Trending videos: when a new outage or incident guide, or a fast-track article scoring 12+, is published, the write job starts `social.yml` (slug, force, a first-second-hook brief) and `longvideo.yml` (slug, force, `publish_now`, upload) with GITHUB_TOKEN: the Short and Reels go out at once as always, and the long video is public on YouTube and the Facebook Page at once instead of 14:00 UTC. At most 1 pair a UTC day (`autoVideo` in `radar/results.jsonl`); consumer topics (online-safety, mobiles, laptops-hardware, how-to-tips) get the Short only (`autoVideoPlan` in `radar/lib.mjs`). A trending long video made before the 05:13 UTC daily run replaces that day's daily video. `dry_run` prints the commands without starting anything.

Email alerts: readers sign up in the "Outage alerts" box near the top of outage guides (or "Security alerts" after incident guides) on www.epifive.com, which puts them in the Resend segment `Alerts: outage` / `Alerts: security` (and the weekly newsletter). When the write job publishes a NEW outage or incident guide and the page is live, `radar.mjs alert` sends one short Resend broadcast to that segment (subject, what is down, the "Status as of" sentence, the first thing to do, the link, unsubscribe, postal footer). 1 alert per incident (radar key, slug, or outage platform family within 3 days), at most 2 in 24 hours of which at most 1 security alert, never for an update or a resolved outage (`alertGate` in `radar/alert.mjs`). Every decision is logged in `radar/results.jsonl` with `"type":"alert"`. Secrets: `RESEND_API_KEY`; optional `ALERT_POSTAL_ADDRESS` (full postal address for the footer). Preview without sending: `node radar/radar.mjs alert --dry-run --site <site checkout> --task task.json --status published --file content/posts/<slug>.md`.

## Comment replies (`replies.yml`, `replies/`)

"Comment GUIDE, get the link": comments asking for the guide (GUIDE, LINK, PDF, CHECKLIST, MIGRATE, DMARC, FIX, QUOTE, plus Hinglish asks such as "link bhejo") on EpiFive's YouTube videos, Facebook Page posts/Reels and Instagram posts/Reels get one friendly reply, signed "EpiFive Team", with the video's own guide or a https://www.epifive.com/get/<keyword> page (UTM tagged; Instagram gets a short typed link `epifive.com/g/<code>` because links in Instagram comments are not clickable). Script: `scripts/social/comment-replies.mjs` in the site repo. It also posts the guide link as the channel's first comment on new public YouTube videos that have none. Runs after each Launch radar run, at most once every 50 minutes; no cron and no scheduler change. Caps: 8 replies a run, 30 per platform a day, one reply per comment and per person per video, never our own comments; a rate limit ends the run and a policy or permission error pauses the platform (Actions > Comment replies > Run workflow with `resume`). Variables: `COMMENT_REPLIES` = on (automatic runs), `COMMENT_REPLY_PLATFORMS` (default youtube,facebook,instagram; drop instagram when ManyChat answers Instagram comments), `META_DM` = on for Meta private replies (needs Advanced Access for people without a role on the app; a permission error switches it off), `COMMENT_FUNNEL_IG` = manychat or dm for "in your DMs" caption wording. Log (counts only, no names or comment texts): `replies/log/<YYYY-MM>.md`; state with salted-hash ids: `replies/state.json`. YouTube has no subscribe gate: YouTube's fake-engagement policy prohibits content that exists to incentivise engagement, and the API cannot see a commenter's subscriptions.

## Daily performance report (`report.yml`, `report/`)

The owner's "aaj ka performance" email, about 20:00 IST every day (Cloudflare scheduler, 14:16 UTC slot): website visits/page views for the IST day so far vs yesterday (Cloudflare Web Analytics; sources Direct/Google/Bing/AI/Facebook/Teams/others, top 5 pages, countries with China flagged as bots), today's articles (site repo `automation/log.md`), YouTube for EpiFive and Shyam Premi SN (subscribers/views vs yesterday's snapshot, uploads of the last 48 h, top video of the week, Analytics day with its 2-3 day lag), Facebook/Instagram followers and the last 5 posts per brand (separate sections), CRM leads, open deals and today's outreach (`/api/ingest/report`), tomorrow's plan (dated backlog item, Shyam's `day-plan.mjs`, scheduled YouTube uploads) and one "aaj ki seekh" line. Resend from hello@epifive.com to info@epifive.com (BCC the owner). Every section is fail-soft. Logs: section statuses and counts only; `report/snapshot.json` keeps public counts (subscribers, views, followers) and the last sent date. Manual: Actions > Daily performance report > Run workflow (`dry_run` prints the email with private lines masked; `force` sends again). Secrets: `RESEND_API_KEY`, `CF_API_TOKEN` (Account Analytics: Read), `SITE_REPO_TOKEN`, `YT_*`, `SHYAM_YT_*`, `META_*`, `IG_USER_ID`, optional `SHYAM_META_PAGE_ID`/`SHYAM_META_PAGE_TOKEN`/`SHYAM_IG_USER_ID`, `INGEST_TOKEN` + var `CRM_URL`. Tests: `node --test report/report.test.mjs`.

## AI Tasks agent (`ai-task.yml`, `ai-task/`)

The owner writes a task in the CRM (AI Tasks screen, owner only): title, free-text prompt, target (EpiFive site, this repo, Shyam Premi SN, the CRM, research only), priority, "approval before publish" and "dry run". The CRM starts `ai-task.yml` (workflow_dispatch with its own fine-grained token, Actions read/write on this repo only). One run = one task: `ai-task/task.mjs claim` takes the next queued task from the CRM (`POST /api/ingest/ai-tasks/claim`, INGEST_TOKEN), the target repo is checked out without stored credentials, `prepare` writes `.ai-task/TASK.md` (task, thread, publishing state, finish contract) and the house rules (site repo `automation/OWNER-RULES.md`), Claude Code works (35 min, 120 turns, a per-target Bash allow-list with no git push/curl/generic node, no secrets in its environment) and writes `.ai-task/result.json`; `collect` reads it, the quality gates run (site `npm run build` with every content check, node tests next to changed files, CRM typecheck + tests), the push step commits only the agent's files and pushes to `main` when publishing is allowed (approved in the CRM) or to branch `ai-task/<id>` for the owner's Approve; allowed follow-up workflows (social.yml, longvideo.yml, article.yml, report.yml, indexnow.yml, control.yml) start only with publishing allowed; `report` posts the result to the CRM and emails the owner a short Hinglish summary (Resend, hello@ -> info@, BCC). A run starts the next one while tasks are queued. "Performance numbers" tasks get `report/report.mjs --out` (the daily report text) in a git-excluded file. THIS REPO IS PUBLIC: the log shows task ids, targets, statuses and counts only; Claude's output is not printed (`show_full_output`/`display_report` off). Secrets: `CLAUDE_CODE_OAUTH_TOKEN`, `INGEST_TOKEN`, `SITE_REPO_TOKEN`, `RESEND_API_KEY`; optional `SHYAM_REPO_TOKEN`, `CRM_REPO_TOKEN`. Tests: `node --test ai-task/ai-task.test.mjs`.

## CRM Control Center snapshots (`control.yml`, `control/`)

`control/snapshot.mjs` builds five JSON snapshots for the CRM's Control Center and posts them to `POST /api/ingest/control`: performance (website today vs yesterday at the same time with China bots apart, sources, top pages, 7-day trend; YouTube EpiFive + Shyam; newsletter count), health (last run of the important workflows in this repo, EPIVIFE and shyam-premi-sn, plus Cloudflare / YouTube / Search Console / Resend / Meta token checks and the LinkedIn expiry variables), content (today's articles, top of the backlog), social (last post per platform, Instagram failures) and shyam (next festivals, owner-footage queue and skip list, email-ingest status). Started by the CRM's Refresh button and after the daily report and social runs (`workflow_run`); no cron. Logs: "<snapshot>: ok|failed" only. Tests: `node --test control/control.test.mjs`.
